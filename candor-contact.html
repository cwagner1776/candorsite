Before you write in

Quick answers.

Most-asked questions
How fast will I get a response?
It depends on the channel. Corrections: under 24 hours. Subject-of-article: under 12 hours. Subscription support: under 4 hours during business hours. General form: usually within a business day. We track these targets monthly and publish the actual numbers in our annual transparency report.
Will you publish my message?
No — not unless you explicitly ask us to. All inbound messages are confidential. The only exception is that significant corrections may be summarized (anonymously, by default) in our public corrections log.
Can I cancel my subscription by email?
Yes. Email editor@candornews.org with the email address on your account and we'll cancel the same day. You can also do it instantly from your account settings — no dark-pattern friction.
I have a story tip — should I send it here?
Candor synthesizes existing reporting rather than originating it, so we're rarely the right destination for primary tips. That said, structural tips — about media practices, source-outlet concerns, or our own coverage — are very welcome at editor@candornews.org.
Do you accept advertising or sponsored content?
We accept a limited amount of display advertising; we don't accept sponsored content, native ads, or affiliate placements anywhere on the site. Advertising inquiries go to editor@candornews.org.
Where can I send physical mail?
We don't have a mailing address listed yet — one is coming soon. In the meantime, email editor@candornews.org and we'll arrange the right way to send anything physical.
