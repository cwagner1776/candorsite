How ratings are determined

Candor does not develop its own bias ratings from scratch. We cross-reference three established independent rating systems and apply our own editorial review to arrive at a final classification. Where the three sources disagree, our editorial team makes the final call and documents the reasoning.

Ratings are reviewed annually for all sources, and on an ad hoc basis when a major editorial change occurs at an outlet — such as a change in ownership, editorial leadership, or documented shift in coverage patterns. If you believe a rating is out of date or incorrect, you can submit a correction request.

Important: Bias ratings describe editorial framing tendencies, not factual accuracy. A left-leaning outlet can publish excellent, accurate journalism. A centrist outlet can make serious factual errors. Candor's confidence scoring is calculated independently from bias classification.
