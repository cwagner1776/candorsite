Founder's note

Why Candor exists.

2026
From the editor · Carson Wagner

You can read the same news event across a left-leaning daily, a centrist wire, and a right-leaning outlet and come away with three different impressions of what happened. The underlying facts are usually almost identical. The framing is not — and the framing is what people end up fighting about.

I started Candor because I think that gap is exactly the kind of work modern AI can do well: read widely across the spectrum, isolate the facts outlets actually agree on, strip the spin, and attribute every claim back to its source — at a scale no individual reader has time for. Used carefully and held to real editorial standards, it can deliver the truth to far more people, on far better terms than the attention economy offers.

Candor is early. It's independent and self-funded, and it's being built in the open — standards, bias ratings, and a public corrections process first, before the pipeline goes fully live. The commitment is simple and it won't change: every claim stays traceable, the AI never runs without a human accountable for it, and when something is wrong, it gets corrected in public.

More to come here as the product takes shape. If you want to follow along or share a thought, reach out.

— Carson Wagner, Founder & Editor-in-Chief
