Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the controls you have. Written to be read, not to be hidden behind.

On this page

§ 01

The short version

Candor is reader-supported, not advertiser-supported. That single fact shapes this entire policy: we have no commercial reason to build a profile of you, sell your behavior, or track you across the web — so we don't.

This page explains, in plain language, the limited data we do handle and the reasons we handle it. The Last updated date for this policy is May 27, 2026.

§ 02

What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run a news service and reply to you:

Account data. If you create an account, we store your name, email address, and authentication details. If you subscribe, our payment processor handles your card details — we never see or store the full card number.

Usage data. We keep aggregate, de-identified metrics about which stories are read and how the AI summary tools are used, so we can improve them. These are not tied to your identity.

Messages you send us. When you use the contact form or submit a correction, we keep what you sent so we can respond and maintain a record.

§ 03

How we use it

We use your data to operate your account, deliver the product you asked for, reply to your messages, and improve the accuracy of our summaries. That is the complete list.

Our commitment

We will never use your reading history to target political content, nor sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for any purpose.

§ 04

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not build cross-site advertising profiles. The only parties who ever touch your data are the service providers strictly required to run Candor — payment processing, email delivery, and hosting — each bound by contract to use it only on our instructions.

§ 05

Cookies & tracking

We use a small number of first-party cookies that are necessary for sign-in and remembering your theme preference. We do not use third-party advertising or social-media tracking cookies. There is no advertising pixel anywhere on Candor.

§ 06

Your rights

Wherever you live, we extend the same core rights to everyone: you can access the data we hold about you, correct it, export it, or ask us to delete it. To exercise any of these, email our team and we will respond within 30 days.

§ 07

Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we delete personal data within 90 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain billing records. De-identified, aggregate metrics may be kept indefinitely because they cannot be linked back to you.

§ 08

Questions about this policy

Privacy questions go straight to a human. Reach us through the contact page and choose "Privacy" as the topic.
